# Klassroom — Feature Roadmap

## Priority Features

1. **Submission grading / comments** — Instructors can leave a grade and written feedback on each submission; students see it on their dashboard.

2. **Announcements** — Instructors can post classroom-wide announcements; students see a notification badge and a feed on their dashboard.

3. **CSV export** — Export the submission matrix (or student list) as a CSV for gradebook import.

4. **Student submission history page** — A dedicated page where a student can see every assignment they've submitted, with dates and links, across all classrooms.

5. **Leaderboard** — Ranked view of students by streak length within a classroom, visible to both instructor and students.

---

## Additional Features

6. **Badges / achievements** — Automatically award badges for milestones (e.g. 7-day streak, first submission, perfect week).

7. **Late submission warnings** — Flag submissions that come in after the assignment due date; surface the flag in the submission matrix.

8. **Assignment templates** — Save a set of fields (title pattern, description, due-date offset) as a reusable template so instructors can create recurring assignments quickly.

9. **Due date reminders** — Send an in-app (or email) reminder to students who haven't submitted 24 hours before a due date.

10. **Assignment feed** — Students see a chronological feed of all upcoming and past assignments across every classroom they're enrolled in.

11. **Progress chart** — A simple line or bar chart on the student dashboard showing submission activity over the past 30 days.

12. **Student self-enroll via link** — Generate a shareable URL (not just a code) that takes a student directly to the join flow for a specific classroom.

13. **Email notifications** — Opt-in email alerts for new assignments, graded submissions, and announcements.

14. **Dark mode** — A toggleable dark color scheme persisted in localStorage.

15. **Mobile PWA** — Add a web-app manifest and service worker so students can install Klassroom as a PWA on their phones.
